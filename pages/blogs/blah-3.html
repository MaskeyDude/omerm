THE DIFFERENCE I'VE YET TO UNDERSTAND...

SAT, 00 JAN AT 00:00 AM

I wonder about the difference between me and them. Between you and them.

REMOVING SEXUAL ASPECTS COMPLETELY

Who are you, who am I, who we are to each other? What makes our partner so different from others? People say many blurry lines overlap you can't really make a line somewhere. Maybe it's only sexual or deep physical affection but I see others then say they also cuddle friends.

maybe some friendships could just be romantic in nature or maybe we all define labels differently?

You being more comfortable than I am with laying your head on people's laps, or shoulders, holding hands, or merely being patted but for me these are things I only associate with you so how come a betrayal wouldn't be felt especially on the same level as a sexual one even when I know it isn't?

emotions, i've learned blurring the logic's sayings. a part of my mind knowing their difference yet another not comprehending; burns me again.

But I wanna learn... for us, myself and you especially.

Is it to breathe the same air as you while we lay down holding hands silently in darkness?

Maybe it's to gently stroke your whole body while holding you closely from the back?

Could it be to slide my fingers across your eyes and face while gazing at the softness of your face?

A therapist can know how my mind works. They learn and then understand it in and out. People other than you I need understanding, love, care, acceptance, and at times like affection from. My friends whom I shared a vulnerable part of me with. For the buddies who I allowed to see me without masks as well now. Having common ground of understanding and acceptance with others for a friendship to develop.

How is it all different with you then? Why is it a feeling of intense bareness in front of you even though a random therapist all the time or even family and friends seeing through me at times but maybe it's cause they don't have that connection I have with you?

To them, It's showing myself to help me understand my mind. For family and friends only a part of it for our connection. And with you, I show every part of me so you understand every bit of me.

Predict me like a story you've read many times. Comprehend me and never have a need to guess what things are or mean to me especially who you are to me ♥︎

In me, there's a deep longing to learn... you. Who are you? What are you made of? What is your core? Not just your desires, hopes, dreams, loved ones, your past stories, your plans, show me how you even think, to understand what your behaviors mean, to reach your buried parts, to figure you out just like I do with myself.

I am my soul and I wanna reach yours. . .

‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ ‎ and feel it, know it, learn to the point of comprehending it, taste it, and let it entangle fully with mine.

Is it to hug someone for so long till we get tired, pressing them so tightly on us that they leave an imprint now even sweating yet still not letting go?

Would understanding complaining of the eyes count? Would feeling the torture of not being understood and loved fully years later count?

How many overlaps do all these connections have? Almost Uncountable.

Human warmth is nice but the cold hands are as if screaming for a different one. A specific skin to be touched by.

Are foreheads touching while laying down something that is only ours? Or is it dancing with closed doors and soft smiles?

To miss our families and friends when they're not around. To think excitedly when finding things they jump around for. Having things only each other understands and giddy around for. To involve them in our lives and desire their peaceful presence for life.

. . . not so different yet there is. To yearn with deep breaths. almost like a deep cry for your being even though we just met merely days ago.

Family makes a place, a home. You are my family while also you are my place, my home. Somewhere I wanna go back to and just lay. As if everything good in my life is a great bustle of joy and you are the glad calm of it.

A calm surrounding you at home after coming back from a long adventure giddy day. A comfortable silence enough to make you tearful.

Does a desire to breathe in their scent count? Don't people like a newborn's scent as well? Then is it a want to feel their body in your arms? Don't people want the feel of kiddos hugging them at times?

So mingled are these thoughts... as if I'm also a child waiting to be held and fighting against his siblings so only I get to be close to my mother.

My whole life seems so normal through the lens of my little self's memories yet when did I become this damaged? How come I have these many injuries and needles in me that are rusted, hurting me when they're touched, moved a little, or maybe an attempt to be plucked out is made?

How safe do you feel with a person? Does comfortability in weird things, joking and poking like siblings, discussing subjects as curious podcasters, adventures like buddies, understanding as if therapists, a protectiveness as if a parent figure, to coddle each other as if children, to battle against conflicts like adults with complex miseries, to be loyal as if a soldier for a country, ... so tangled are all these things.

Many of these mixtures overlapping and appearing for others as well yet so differently appearing for each and especially for you.

To be the other half? Maybe we're already complete. To be appreciated for our completeness fully. Inch by inch not just kissing the moles of your body it's also to gaze lovingly at the parts of you so carelessly and comfortably coming out.

The center of each other's lives? My world? My everything? Maybe we already have that something. We're already the center of our lives. Maybe it's to stand beside each other in that center. To dance in the main character spotlight we have on us. To place an empty seat beside ours; Maybe the one we call our throne too hehe.

To hold hands and take each step together. While others walk in different paces. It's that pause for a bit. It's the carrying of each other when one of us can't walk so we keep going forward together.

Like two different textured roads blending and us walking on that path, showing each other the gardens full of flowers. Pretty, with thorns, so fresh, growing, dead, suffering, oldest, the new seeds, the ones who never grew... and expecting each other to not burn instead to support in flourishing them. The areas many don't know exists, others only know that it does, some may see a curtain while for a few they can through a glass barrier yet we let each other walk in it and even touch it to change it as we can.

Not only for one how about for all of our parts even the ones we're planning, building, and even ones we destroyed to nothingness.

Letting them roam everywhere while also being scared of how the mess would be seen like by them or what if the pots you so carefully made were to be broken.

Eyes desperately looking for theirs, hands searching for theirs, to smell their scent, to hear that softness of a voice, a yearn to be touched by that exact warm skin at times like a scream of the body, to let the senses know that being a distinct aura is here almost as if even the essence of theirs needs to be around yours...

It's gentle, their laughs, care, affection, adoration, and it's love. With us all of it as well yet there is one more. That deep intimacy.

What is it? How do you explain it? Not just put it to words instead learn it... To hear the difference of romantic love as being almost indescribable yet wanting badly to describe it.

Teach it to me why don't you? It's gotten lonely to try to resolve it and not being able to then.

Parents and siblings like foundational connections building us since childhood. Friends and buddies helping us broaden our minds and making us grow while bantering around.

You and Me. Us? The one we walk with while sharing every aspect of our life with? Either through direct contact or indirectly through detailed long sentences and random embarrassing little pictures.

Coming back, sitting down, talking through it, handling the urge to not suppress like we usually do to make peace with many people, resolving even the roots of that conflict... not once instead a consistent choice to be made even every moment of the day at times.
